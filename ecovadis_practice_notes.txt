SUPPLIER NETWORK EXPORT — INTERNAL NOTES
(practice dataset for interview simulation)

Source: this file is a raw export merged from our supplier self-reporting
portal (versions portal_v1 and portal_v2) plus a legacy manual import batch,
covering roughly the last three fiscal years. Companies self-report their
own identity and the identity of any known suppliers.

Known data quality caveats (non-exhaustive):
- Company names are free text — expect inconsistent capitalization,
  spacing, legal-suffix formatting, and occasional typos or encoding
  artifacts carried over from older CSV exports.
- Tax/VAT ID formats differ by country and are sometimes missing entirely.
  A missing ID does not necessarily mean "no relationship" — it may simply
  mean the reporting company didn't provide it.
- Country names appear in mixed formats (full name, ISO code, or
  local-language spelling) depending on which system generated the row.
- Some rows are duplicate or re-submitted records from different
  reporting periods.
- Not every row is relevant to the specific network we care about — this
  export includes unrelated supplier relationships bundled in from other
  client accounts.
- A small number of rows are structurally broken (missing identifying
  fields) and were not cleaned before export.

Anchor company for this exercise: NorthGate Textiles S.A. (Spain) — this
is our directly onboarded client. Map their supplier network outward
(tier 1, tier 2, tier 3...) as far as the data allows.

Columns:
- row_id: raw record identifier (no business meaning)
- source_system: which system produced this row
- reported_by / reported_by_tax_id / reported_by_country: the company
  submitting this record
- declared_supplier / declared_supplier_tax_id / declared_supplier_country:
  the supplier they declared
- relationship_type: free-text category as entered by the reporter
- last_updated: when the reporting company last confirmed this record
  (date format varies by source system)
- notes: free-text field, usually empty
