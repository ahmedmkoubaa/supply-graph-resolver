Corporate Registry Notes (companion reference, cross-check against the customs extract)

1. Solstice Materials Europe GmbH (DE) also trades under the name "Bergwerk Mining Alias GmbH"
   following an internal group restructuring. Same legal entity, same corporate group as
   Solstice Materials Co. Both names appear on shipping manifests in this period.

2. Solstice Analytics Inc (US) has no group or ownership relationship to Solstice Materials Co.
   The shared "Solstice" name is coincidental: it is an independent laboratory-testing vendor,
   founded by former employees under an unrelated holding company.

3. Entities whose shipped goods are generic packaging, freight, or logistics services (rather
   than a named material or chemical input) are usually customs brokers or freight forwarders
   passing goods through on behalf of the actual buyer or seller. Don't count them as material
   Tier-N suppliers unless you have separate evidence they manufacture something.
